🧟 ZOMBIE CARD WARS 🧟

Survive the undead horde with your deck of cards!

How to Play

🃏 Click cards to play them (spend Brain Energy)

⚡ Each turn you gain energy and draw cards

🧟 Survive waves of zombies — they attack if not killed!

🛡️ Defense blocks damage | ❤️ Heal restores HP

🔥 AoE cards hit ALL zombies at once

💀 Bosses appear every 5 waves!

Press SPACE to End Turn
