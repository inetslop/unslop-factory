MINESWEEPER DUEL

Two-Player Competitive Minesweeper

How to Play

🎮 Players take turns revealing cells on a shared board.

⚡ If you reveal a MINE, you LOSE the round!

🏆 The player who survives when all safe cells are cleared WINS!

🚩 Flag cells to mark suspected mines.

⚠️ Hit a mine and your opponent wins instantly.

Left Click: Reveal | Right Click: Flag

P1: Blue
P2: Green
