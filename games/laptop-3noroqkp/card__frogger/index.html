FROGGER CARDS

A strategic card game where you guide frogs across a dangerous road using movement cards to reach the lily pads.
HOW TO PLAY:
• You have 5 frogs. Get each to the top row (lily pads) to win.
• Each turn, draw cards and play one to move your frog.
• Red cells are car hazards — if your frog ends a turn on one, it's hit!
• SHIELD cards protect from one car hit.
• SKIP lets you wait and redraw.
• Cars reposition each turn. Plan ahead!

CONTROLS:
Click a card to play it • Keys 1-4 select hand cards • R restarts

GAME OVER

Score: 0 Frogs: 🐸×5 Turn: 1
Play a card to move your frog.
