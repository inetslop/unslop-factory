Tetris Minesweeper

Drop the shapes. Read the numbers. Bury every bomb before your blocks run dry.

  • PLACE — click / tap a cell to drop the active tetromino. R or right-click rotates it.
  • SCAN — every block you lay reveals the bomb-count around it. A 0 is guaranteed safe ground.
  • SURVIVE — blocks are limited. Cover all the bombs, or go dry and lose the grid.
