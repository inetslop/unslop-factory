**How it plays:** Letters rain down onto a 9×13 board. A word glows at the bottom — type it, and every tile on the board holding one of its letters lights up gold and shatters when you finish, with gravity pulling the rest down. New drops are biased to feed you the letters you still need, so words stay completable. Chain clears inside 6 seconds to climb from ×1 to ×4 combo; a clean word (no misses) earns a +40 bonus. If any column reaches the red overflow line, it's game over — score, accuracy, and top combo are tallied, with best score saved locally. `P` pauses, `M` mutes, `R` restarts from the pause/game-over screens.
