GEM DUEL

One keyboard. Two rivals. Chain the cascade.

Take turns swapping adjacent gems on a shared board. Match 3 or more to score — cascades multiply points and hand you another turn. A failed swap gives the board to your rival. First to 800 wins. Your swap always fires toward the cell you last moved into (watch your chevron).
