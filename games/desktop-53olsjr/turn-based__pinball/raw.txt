**How it plays:** every flipper flick spends one of your 10 turns. Bumpers, slings, the spinner and rollover lanes all score multiplied by a chain that grows with every hit you make without flipping — hourglass targets give +2 turns, the golden orb +3, lighting all three top lanes gives +1. You have 3 nudges per shot before a TILT ends it, and if your turns run out the ball starts fading on a visible countdown ring. Hold **Space** to charge the plunger, release to launch; **A/←** and **D/→** flip, **W/↑** nudges, **R** restarts any time, **M** toggles sound. Your best single-ball score is saved between sessions.
