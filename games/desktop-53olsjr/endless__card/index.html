The Endless Deck · An Endless Suit-Rush

CARD ♥ STORM

Cards rain from the void over the dealer's table. Catch only the suit you're called — miss one, or grab a wrong card, and the house takes a heart.

How to Play

  • Catch every card of the called suit before it crosses the dealer's line.
  • Wrong cards cost a heart — 3 hearts and you're out.
  • Streaks raise your ×multiplier. The deck falls faster as you climb.
  • Gold Jokers: +75 pts and a shield that saves one mistake.
