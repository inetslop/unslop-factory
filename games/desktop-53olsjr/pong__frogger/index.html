PONG FROGGER

SMACK THE BALLS · DODGE THE FLAMES · REACH THE LILY LINE

HOW TO PLAY

  • Hop between the lanes — time your jumps like Frogger.
  • Touch a ball to PONG it back. Every smack makes it faster and pays more points.
  • After 4 smacks a ball ignites. A burning ball kills you — give it room!
  • Ride the drifting lily pads across the water. No pad = splash.
  • Hit the glowing HOME line before the clock runs out to start a faster wave.
