SHOOTERPONG

Classic rally, lethal paddles. Out-score the machine to 7 — or blast its paddle off the board. Every bolt that clips the ball drags it down; every hex cell you hit is a weapon upgrade.

CONTROLS

  • WS / ↑↓  move paddle
  • SPACE  fire blaster (hold for auto)
  • P pause  ·  M mute  ·  R restart

BATTLEFIELD INTEL

  • Shots that hit the incoming ball slow it down — play defense with your gun.
  • Paddle integrity is 4 hits. At zero, the match ends instantly.
  • Hit a hex cell with a bolt: TRI-SHOT or HEAVY ROUNDS.
