Snake Golf

Your snake is the caddie. Charge a putt, watch it roll, then slither back up to fetch the ball — and sink nine holes under par if you can.

Rules of Play

  • ←↑↓→Steer the caddie. (WASD works too.)
  • hold SPACECharge the club — the meter swings up and back, so time it.
  • release SPACESwing! One stroke per putt. A dashed line previews your roll.
  • walk onto itA loose ball is picked up the moment your head reaches it.
  • 💧 water+1 penalty stroke; the ball returns to the bank where it splashed in.
  • 🪱 wormsEat them: you grow longer… and gain club power. Tangle yourself and you shed a few segments.
  • R P MReplay hole · pause · mute.
