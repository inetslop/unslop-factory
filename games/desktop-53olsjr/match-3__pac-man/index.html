HOW TO PLAY

Swap two dots to line up 3+ of a color — Pac-Man chomps them. Reach the level target before your moves run out!

4 IN A ROW → GHOST BOMB: explodes a 3×3 area when matched. Sometimes it leaves behind a PAC TILE — wild, matches anything, chomps its neighbors.

L / T SHAPE or 5+ in a row → POWER PELLET: wipes every dot of its color. Beware: ghosts raid the maze every few seconds!

CONTROLS

MOUSE / TOUCH — click & drag a dot toward its neighbor, or tap two dots next to each other.

ARROW KEYS — move Pac-Man. Every step swaps with the dot you face (invalid swaps bounce back and cost nothing).

SPACE — trigger PAC FRENZY when the gauge is full: chomp 14 dots anywhere on the board.
