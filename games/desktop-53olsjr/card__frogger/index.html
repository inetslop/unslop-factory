FROGGER


CARDS

A lane-crossing card duel. Play a card — the world shifts. Ride logs, out-time traffic, and claim all five burrows.

ENTER start  ·  1–5 play cards  ·  Arrows / WASD quick hops  ·  R restart  ·  M sound

The Playbook

  • One card = one hop. After every card the lanes advance by their speed — plan ahead, your hand is your compass.
  • Gold chevrons mark each lane's direction & speed. Two chevrons = fast. Cross roads through the gaps.
  • Ride logs and ducks — they carry you with the current. Drift off the edge and it's over.
  • RIPPLE freezes your lane for one shift · MIST shields you through the next shift · LEAP hops two squares, skipping a whole lane.
  • Fill all 5 burrows to win. Filling them in order pays double. You have three lives — choose your cards wisely.
