**How it plays:** you're a sapper running across procedurally dug minefields. Every green tile hides either a classic Minesweeper number (counting the mines touching it) or a spike — land on it to dig it up, and zero-tiles chain-dig themselves open. Use the numbers to route around hidden mines, mark suspects with ↓/F/right-click, ride supply crates across pits, grab gems, and reach the flag before your 3 lives run out. The field re-randomizes (with a solvability check) after every wipe, and `R` restarts the whole run anytime.
