HOW TO PLAY
  • Swap adjacent balls to line up 3 or more — each popped ball sinks toward the flag.
  • Finish under your par strokes for BIRDIE & EAGLE on the card.
  • 4-in-a-row → STRIPE ball clears its whole line · 5+ → ACE wipes a color off the course.
