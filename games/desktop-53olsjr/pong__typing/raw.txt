**How it plays:** Your paddle is pure muscle memory — every letter you type correctly pours INK into the meter, and while ink lasts your paddle sprints to track the ball. Miss a letter and you stumble; Backspace erases it for free. The CPU types too (you can watch its word progress top-right), and when it throws a typo it freezes mid-stroke — out-type it to first-to-7. WPM, accuracy, rally streaks and a saved personal best round out the match report.
