PRISM DELVE

A turn-based match-three descent into the crystal shaft. Forge specials, chain cascades, and clear the quota before your moves run dry.

Field Notes

  • Swap two adjacent gems to line up 3 or more.
  • Match 4 → forge a PRISM — swap it to clear its whole row & column.
  • Match 5 or an L/T shape → forge a NOVA star — swap it with a gem to blast every gem of that colour.
  • Cascades multiply your score. Blast a special into a blast and the whole mine shakes.
  • Leftover moves pay +50 gold each when you clear the depth.
