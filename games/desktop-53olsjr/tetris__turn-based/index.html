TURNTAKES

A turn-based take on tetris. No gravity, no timer — every line is earned by placement alone.

  • Take your time. Slide, step and rotate a piece as long as you like — it never falls on its own.
  • Lock to resolve. Press SPACE: full rows clear instantly and the well collapses down.
  • Plan ahead. You see 3 pieces ahead (7-bag). Clear every turn to grow your CHAIN bonus.
  • Lock in ≤ 6 moves for a SWIFT bonus. When a fresh piece can't fit at the top, the well is full — game over.
