**GRAVITON** is a full Tetris with a twist: press **G** to invert gravity. Pieces rise, the sky becomes your floor, lines collapse upward — and every clear pays ×2. You start with 2 charges (max 4), earning one per 3 lines cleared. Classic features are all there: 7-bag, hold, 3-piece preview, SRS kicks, ghost piece, lock delay, combos, back-to-back TETRIS bonus, and a gravity-themed speed curve — idle pieces literally accelerate until you move them. The whole scene reacts to the flip: starfield drifts upward, the palette shifts from cyan deep-space to amber heat, particles fall (or rise) with the field, and hard drops shake the board. Sound is synthesized live via Web Audio after your first input; best score persists locally. **R** restarts anytime, no reload needed.
