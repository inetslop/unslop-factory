MINEQUAKE

PHYSICS MINESWEEPER. Rack up power and fire the steel ball into the grid — hard shots punch through cover plates, soft taps crack & ricochet. Read the etched numbers, pin the mines, sweep the rest. One graze of a buried mine ends the run.
