MINESWEEPERGOLF

Read the numbers. Dodge the booms. Sink in par.

COURSE NOTES

  • Drag from the ball (or aim with keys) and release to swing. Every shot is a stroke.
  • Rolling cuts the turf and reveals digits — each one counts the mines next door, just like classic Minesweeper.
  • High digits are rough, shock-grass that slows your ball. A 0 cascades open into fast fairway.
  • Land on a mine: BOOM, +1 penalty, and you're back where you swung from.
