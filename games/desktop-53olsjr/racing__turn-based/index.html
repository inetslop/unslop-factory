APEXTURN GP

A turn-based grand prix. One decision per round, four cars on the line, three laps to the flag.

RACE BRIEFING

  • ▸Every round you play one card — keys 1–5 or click. All four cars move together.
  • ▸Corners have a speed limit. Steer into the bend or slide wide — off-track costs you the turn.
  • ▸Hit a corner at ≥4.5 SPD while steering in for an APEX DRIFT: +1 bonus distance.
  • ▸Nitro is a straight-line weapon — 3 charges, +1 every lap. No contact between cars.
