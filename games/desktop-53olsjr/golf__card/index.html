BIRDIE NINE

NINE HOLES · ONE DECK · PAR 38

HOW TO PLAY

  • Each hole has a distance and a wind. Your five cards are your bag of clubs — every rank has fixed yardage.
  • Play a card to send the ball that many yards (plus the wind). Cards that would cross the pin can't be played.
  • Land within 6 yd and it drops. Within 25 yd you're on the green: play the gold PUTT card for exactly what's left.
  • Stuck? Redraw your hand — it costs one stroke. Fewest strokes over nine holes beats par 38.

CONTROLS

  • CLICK / TAP a card — swing
  • 1 – 5 play cards  ·  P putt
  • SPACE / D redraw hand (+1 stroke)
  • R restart run  ·  M sound on/off
