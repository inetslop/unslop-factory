The War Table

A top-down duel of cards & crests — all you see is felt, wood and war.

Deploy. Spend energy to place units in the three lanes (max 3). New units join behind your line; left number is strike, right is vitality.
Clash. End your turn: each lane's front unit strikes the enemy's front — if it survives, it counters. An open lane lands a blow on the enemy crest.
Archers. Bow-marked units fire over melee and never take counters. Break the enemy crest (20) before yours shatters.
