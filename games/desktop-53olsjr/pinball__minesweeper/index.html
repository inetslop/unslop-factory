How to play

  • Every hidden tile hides a number — or a mine. Bounce the ball to blast all the mines; direct hits chain-detonate neighbours.
  • A revealed number counts its adjacent mines. Clear those and the safe tiles around it auto-pop in a cascade.
  • The field persists across all 3 balls. Drain the last one before the board is cleared and it's over.
