**Fairway Duel** is a complete two-player mini-golf showdown. Red aims with **A/D** and putts with **SPACE**; Blue uses **←/→** and **ENTER** (hold to charge, release to strike — power ping-pongs so timing matters). You can also slingshot your ball with the mouse or finger. Three hand-built holes — a sand-guarded fairway, an L-bend, and a spinning windmill paddle — plus automatic playoff holes if totals tie. It features full rolling physics (friction, wall bounces, lip-ins, sand traps), a live paper scorecard, turn indicator on the flag, particles, screen shake, Web Audio sound effects, pause (**ESC**), and instant restart — all in one self-contained file.
