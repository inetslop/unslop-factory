GRIDROCKETS

You bank on energy. The rocks bank on time. Out-think the swarm, one turn at a time.

MISSION BRIEF

  • ◈You get 3 energy per turn — thrust, fire and brace each cost one. Rotating is free.
  • ✦Your shot flies straight up to 4 cells and strikes the first rock in its path.
  • ▲End your turn and every rock drifts toward you — small ones twice as fast. Brace to survive a hit.
  • ⚡Big rocks split into two medium, then two small. Clear a wave to patch +1 hull.
