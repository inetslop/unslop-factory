GEM STREAM

Gems flow from right to left.
Click a gem, then a neighbor (or tap) to swap them.
Line up 3 or more of the same color to pop them and score.
⚠ Dark spiked gems will cost you a life if they scroll off the left edge — match them first!
The stream speeds up as your level rises.
