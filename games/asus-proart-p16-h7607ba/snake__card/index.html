SNAKE

A RISKY CARD GAME OF NERVE

Each player starts with a hidden card that sets their total.
Players take turns: DRAW a card from the deck (adding its value to the total) or PASS the turn. Face cards = 10, Ace = 1.

If your draw makes the total exceed 21, or match any previous total, you are out! The last player standing wins the round.

Be careful: everyone can pass, but if everyone passes, draws become forced.
