🧟 ZOMBIE CARD GAME 🧟
Survive 5 waves of the undead horde
HOW TO PLAY
Each turn you get 3 ⚡ energy and draw a card.
Click a card (or press its 1–5 key) to play it. Cards cost energy.
Deal damage to wipe out the zombie before it eats you alive.
🛡️ Block soaks hits  •  🔩 Nails stun  •  🪙 Coin draws cards
Press E or click END TURN to let the zombie attack.
Survive all 5 waves to win. Lose all HP and... you're food. 🍽️
