GRID RACER

A RACING PUZZLE

Build the track to connect START to FINISH, then race!
• Left click : place piece / rotate placed piece
• Right click : remove piece
• 1 / 2 : pick straight / curve (watch your piece limit!)
• SPACE : race when the track is complete
• R : restart level  • ENTER : next level
Boost tiles /// make your car fly. Finish fast!
