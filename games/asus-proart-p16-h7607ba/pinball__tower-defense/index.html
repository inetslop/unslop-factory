PINBALL DEFENSE

— a tower-defense pinball hybrid —
A / D or ← → — flippers   •   SPACE — launch ball
1 — select Bouncer tower (50 gems)   2 — select Cannon tower (75 gems)
Left-click — build tower   Right-click — demolish (half refund)
P — pause   R — restart

Launch the ball into the descending invaders. Bouncers fling your ball back up.
Cannons auto-fire on enemies. Don't let them reach your BASE!
