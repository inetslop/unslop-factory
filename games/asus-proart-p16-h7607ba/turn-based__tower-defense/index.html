TURN TOWER

A turn-based tower defense

Mouse click select a tower, then place it on grass
1 / 2 / 3 / 4 pick Gun · Cannon · Frost · Sniper
Space or E end your turn (towers fire, enemies move)
U / X upgrade / sell the selected tower
Esc or right-click cancel placement / deselect
R restart after victory or defeat
Enemies crawl along the road one step per turn.
Earn gold from kills. Survive all 15 waves.

Build: Click a placed tower to upgrade / sell
