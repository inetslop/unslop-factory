SILENT KEYS

A typing stealth game.

Type the displayed word. Every correct letter makes you take one step toward the vault.
Guards patrol the corridor with a sight radius — you are only visible to them while you are moving (right after a keystroke).

STILL = hidden. When a guard drifts close, stop typing and let it pass. A wrong letter makes noise: nearby guards become alert and see much farther.
If a guard spots you while you move for too long, the alarm sounds — game over.
